\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:con}

We presented \method, a learning-based framework that casts transistor
network synthesis as a safety-masked sequential graph-generation MDP.
A single four-head policy operates on a unified graph that fuses the partial
network with a fixed SOP encoding of the target, placing transistors under
per-head masks that make every trajectory correct and safe by construction.
Three-phase training---NLL pretraining, PPO with a GRPO baseline and a
self-tightening reward, and a physical-aware phase that rewards the joint
PDN/PUN cell by its ASTRAN placement cost---drives the same policy from
imitation toward minimum transistor count and layout quality.
On the exhaustive three-variable sweep the policy matches the exact SAT
optimum on all $508$ targets, extends to four- and six-variable functions,
and lowers mean placement cost by $18\%$/$15\%$ over count-optimal cells---all
without a per-instance solver call at inference.
