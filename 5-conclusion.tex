\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:con}

We presented \method, which casts transistor network synthesis as a
safety-masked sequential graph-generation MDP and solves it with a four-head
policy over a unified graph.  Three-phase training---NLL pretraining, GRPO-based transistor-count RL,
and a physical-aware ASTRAN-reward phase---matches the exact optimum on all
$508$ three-variable targets, scales to four and six variables, and cuts
placement cost $18\%$/$15\%$ below count-optimal cells, with no solver call at
inference.
